Theme

AI Infrastructure

The investment question is increasingly about bottlenecks. Chips matter, but so do electricity connections, cooling, construction, networks and the ability to finance large physical assets.

Cybersecurity

Security spending follows the value of the systems being protected. AI makes the question more strategic because models, agents and data can sit inside critical business workflows.

Industrial Technology

The most interesting technology businesses often improve an existing physical process. Software becomes valuable when it changes throughput, quality, inventory, energy use or labour productivity.

Telecommunications

The digital economy still runs over physical networks. Fibre, spectrum, towers and edge infrastructure create long-lived assets with strategic importance.

Technology Markets

Technology markets change when a technical improvement changes the economics of an industry. We look for those transitions rather than trying to predict every new product.
