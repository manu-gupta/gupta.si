Cybersecurity

AI is increasing the speed of both attack and defence. The strategic question is how security architecture changes when systems can interpret instructions, generate code and operate continuously.

Infrastructure

The AI stack has a physical layer. Power availability, data-centre construction, cooling, networking and semiconductor supply can determine how quickly digital ambition becomes real capacity.
